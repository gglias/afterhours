    questions

    FAQ

    Why "after hours"?

    I work on these projects in the evenings, Athens time (UTC+2 / UTC+3). Because everything is async, your time zone doesn't matter. Often the work is ready when you wake up.

    What do you need from me to start?

    A link to the site, a test build (TestFlight or APK) or a Figma file. Plus a few lines about your users and what you want to improve. I confirm the scope and the delivery date in writing before you pay.

    How do I pay?

    Bank transfer or card via a payment link. Quick Scan is paid upfront. Bigger projects are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.

    Do you sign NDAs?

    Yes. Send yours, or I can send a simple one. Nothing you share ends up in my portfolio without your OK.

    My product isn't in English. Is that OK?

    Yes. I work in English and Greek. For other languages, I review the structure and flows, and you check the copy.

    Can you also build what you design?

    For websites, yes: Figma → WordPress with Elementor. For apps, I hand off a detailed spec to your developers and stay around for questions.
